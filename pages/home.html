Seasonal Recommendations

Wines express themselves differently as the climate changes. Discover our handpicked selections tailored for each season.

Spring

Petal & Peach Riesling

Rheingau, Germany

Crisp white peach, green apple, and wild elderflower blossom aromas. A bright refreshing palate suited for blooming spring days.

Summer

Ocean Coastarica Rosé

Provence, France

Bone-dry wild strawberry, red currant, and flinty sea-salt minerals. Crafted for bright afternoons and sunset courtyard sips.

Autumn

Harvest Chardonnay

Marlborough, NZ

Toasted hazelnut, vanilla bean, and baked apple pie aromas. Aged in light French oak barrels for a rich, buttery harvest finish.

Winter

Midnight Spice Syrah

Barossa Valley, AU

Deep black bramble fruit, cracked black pepper, tobacco leaf, and fine leather notes. Full-bodied for cozy candlelit winter evenings.

Producer Spotlight

Behind the Bottle

We travel directly to boutique family estates and high-altitude slopes to find wines with a soul, respecting traditions and native ecosystems.

The Alpine Biodynamic Ridge

Located in Valais, Switzerland, our producer cultivates Chasselas grapes on dramatic 45-degree terraced stone walls without chemical fertilizers.

Chalk Cliffs Estate

In Champagne, France, ancient marine fossil soils feed deep roots, giving our sparkling selections their unique chalk complexity.

Volcanic Slopes Cultivation

Grown in dark volcanic ash slopes of Mt. Etna, Italy. High mineral extraction generates structured tannins and peppery finishes.
