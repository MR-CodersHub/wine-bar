Privacy & Security

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to Vintara. This policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and manage your information across our luxury wine platform and experiences.

Last Updated: May 18, 2026

Your Data & Trust

Vintara respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information shared through our website, memberships, reservations, wine collections, and digital experiences.

This Privacy Policy outlines how information is collected, stored, processed, and protected. Humans continue placing their entire lives online, so the least platforms can do is pretend to behave responsibly.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information when you create an account, place orders, reserve experiences, subscribe to newsletters, or interact with Vintara services.

  • Name, email address, and contact information.
  • Payment and billing information.
  • Reservation and shipping details.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected through the platform helps us improve experiences, process reservations, personalize recommendations, and maintain platform security.

  • Processing wine orders and event reservations.
  • Delivering newsletters and curated luxury content.
  • Improving customer support and personalization.

3. Cookies & Tracking

Vintara uses cookies and similar technologies to improve browsing performance, analyze usage trends, and customize experiences.

These technologies help remember preferences and optimize luxury browsing experiences across devices.

4. Payment Security

Payment transactions are processed through secure third-party payment providers using encrypted technologies.

  • Sensitive payment data is encrypted during transmission.
  • Vintara does not store full card details directly.

5. Data Sharing

Personal information is never sold to third parties. Data may only be shared with trusted service providers required for platform operations, payment processing, shipping, or legal compliance.

Unlike certain corners of the internet, we are not interested in turning your browsing habits into a commodity marketplace.

6. Account Protection

Users are responsible for maintaining account confidentiality, including passwords and access credentials.

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Notify us immediately regarding suspicious account activity.

7. User Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, users may have rights regarding access, correction, deletion, or portability of personal information.

Requests regarding personal data may be submitted directly through our support channels.

8. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill legal, operational, and service-related purposes.

Some information may remain archived for compliance, accounting, or fraud prevention obligations.

9. Policy Updates

Vintara reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be reflected on this page with updated effective dates.

10. Contact Us

For privacy concerns, legal inquiries, or requests regarding your personal information, please contact our support team.
